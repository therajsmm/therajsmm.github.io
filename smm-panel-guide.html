What Is an SMM Panel?

An SMM panel is an online dashboard where users can purchase and manage social media marketing services. Depending on the provider, available services may cover Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Telegram and other platforms.

How Do SMM Panels Work?

A customer normally selects a service, enters the required information such as a public post or profile URL, chooses a quantity and submits an order.

The provider then processes the order through its service delivery system. Delivery speed and service characteristics depend on the particular provider and service.

What Should You Consider Before Ordering?

  • Service quality and description
  • Delivery speed
  • Pricing
  • Support availability
  • Refund and replacement policies
  • Order tracking
